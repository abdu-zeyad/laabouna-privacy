Privacy Policy in English

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Laabouna does not collect or send any personal information about your child or you. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

About the app

Laabouna is an Arabic game for children aged 3 to 6, with cooking, coloring and picture puzzles.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-in. We never ask for a name, email, phone number, age or location.
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifier.
  • No ads, no analytics or measurement tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No chat and no contact with other players.

What stays on the device

Progress (stars, coins, achievements), the chosen character, settings and colored pictures are stored only on the device and never reach us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Saving pictures to Photos

On iPhone and iPad, tapping save adds the picture to the Photos app. The system asks for add-only permission; the app never reads your photos or sends them anywhere.

In-app purchase

The app is free with one optional one-time purchase, "Unlock all of Laabouna". Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email or payment details; the device only receives the store's confirmation to unlock the content.

Internet

All play works offline. The app contacts the store only to buy, restore a purchase, or rate the app.

Children's privacy

Purchases, ratings and external links are behind a parental gate (a math question young children cannot answer). Because we collect no personal information from children, there is no data that needs parental consent or deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its date. No data-collecting feature will be added before this policy is updated.
