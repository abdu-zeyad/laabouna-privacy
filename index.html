المعلومات اللي بتضل على الجهاز بس

تقدّم الطفل (النجوم، العملات، الإنجازات)، والشخصية اللي اختارها، والإعدادات، والرسومات اللي لوّنها، كلها بتنحفظ على الجهاز نفسه وما بتوصلنا. لما تحذفوا اللعبة بتنحذف هاي المعلومات.

حفظ الرسومات في الصور (آيفون وآيباد)

إذا كبستوا زر الحفظ، اللعبة بتحفظ الرسمة في تطبيق الصور على الجهاز. الجهاز بيطلب إذن «إضافة صور» بس. اللعبة ما بتقرأ صوركم ولا بترسلها لأي مكان.

اللعبة مجانية، وفيها شراء واحد لمرة وحدة اسمه «افتحوا كل لعبونة». الدفع بيتم بالكامل عن طريق متجر Apple App Store أو Google Play، وحسب سياسة الخصوصية الخاصة بكل متجر. إحنا ما بنستلم اسمكم ولا إيميلكم ولا معلومات الدفع. الجهاز بيستلم من المتجر تأكيد إنه الشراء تم، عشان يفتح المحتوى.

Privacy Policy — Laabouna

Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: Laabouna does not collect or send any personal information about your child or you. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Laabouna is an Arabic game for children aged 3 to 6, with cooking, coloring and picture puzzles. Privacy contact: abdalmajeed76543@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

  • There are no accounts or sign-in. We never ask for a name, email, phone number, age or location.
  • We do not use device identifiers or the advertising identifier.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or measurement tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • There is no chat and no sharing with other players.

What stays on the device

Your child's progress (stars, coins, achievements), chosen character, settings and colored pictures are stored only on the device and are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Saving pictures to Photos (iPhone and iPad)

When you tap save, the picture is added to the device's Photos app. The system asks for add-only permission. The app never reads your photos or sends them anywhere.

In-app purchase

The app is free with one optional one-time purchase, "Unlock all of Laabouna". Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. The device only receives the store's confirmation so it can unlock the content.

Internet

All play works offline. The app contacts the store only when you buy, restore a purchase, or rate the app.

Children's privacy

Purchases, ratings and external links are behind a parental gate (a math question young children cannot answer). Because we collect no personal information from children, there is nothing to obtain consent for or to delete. Questions are welcome at the email above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Any change that would collect data requires updating this policy first.
